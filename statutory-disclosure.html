3. PPA Section 54 Trust Banking Account Declaration

The Property Practitioners Act 22 of 2019 imposes strict statutory fiduciary duties regarding the administration and receipt of trust monies:

  • Zero Trust Monies via Portal: The website rohanrealty.co.za maintains no independent bank account, trust account, or payment processing facility. No client deposits, reservation monies, or trust funds are received, accepted, or processed through this digital portal.
  • Institutional Trust Account Governance: Any and all earnest money deposits, buyer holding funds, or transaction monies relating to agreements concluded through Blue Ocean Realty (Pty) Ltd are held and administered exclusively by:
    • The appointed conveyancing attorneys' audited trust account (governed under Section 86(4) of the Legal Practice Act 28 of 2014); or
    • Blue Ocean Realty (Pty) Ltd’s dedicated, separately audited Trust Banking Account maintained in strict compliance with Section 54 of the Property Practitioners Act 22 of 2019.

5. FICA Compliance (Financial Intelligence Centre Act)

Blue Ocean Realty (Pty) Ltd is an Accountable Institution listed under Schedule 1 of the Financial Intelligence Centre Act (Act No. 38 of 2001, as amended).

As required by law, we enforce rigorous Risk Management and Compliance Programmes (RMCP). Prior to concluding any transaction or mandate, we are legally mandated to establish and verify the identity of all natural persons, trusts, and legal entities, identify beneficial owners, and report suspicious or unusual transactions to the Financial Intelligence Centre (FIC).
