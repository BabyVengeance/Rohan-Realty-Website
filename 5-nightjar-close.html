Property Overview

Architectural Grandeur in Hawaan Forest Estate

Experience the pinnacle of luxury living in this extraordinary 5-bedroom residence, perfectly positioned on a rare double plot within one of South Africa's most prestigious eco-estates.

Culinary Excellence

Gourmet Caesarstone Kitchen & Cold Room

At the heart of the home is a spectacular gourmet Caesarstone kitchen equipped with premium integrated appliances, a centre island, gas hob, eye-level oven, separate scullery, and laundry. Distinctive luxury highlights include a dedicated walk-in refrigerated cold room, integrated refrigerators, and multiple coffee stations for morning coffee and refined hospitality.

Walk-In Cold Room Integrated Appliances
Entertaining & Leisure

Double-Volume Flow, Wine Cellar & Pool

A grand double-volume entrance welcomes you into elegant living spaces where floor-to-ceiling glass stack-back doors open effortlessly onto a sparkling swimming pool and landscaped entertainment area. The social wing features a stylish dry bar, a temperature-controlled air-conditioned wine cellar, and a captivating large tropical glass aquarium.

Air-Conditioned Wine Cellar Tropical Aquarium & Bar
Estate Grounds & Self-Sufficiency

Private Guest Wing, Double Stand & Inverter

Situated on a rare 2,195 m² double stand, this residence offers unmatched privacy with elevated vistas of the Indian Ocean and indigenous coastal forest. The layout includes an expansive master retreat and a private self-contained guest wing with its own lounge, dressing room, and direct pool access. Complete peace of mind is assured with a high-capacity inverter system, backup water tanks, automated blinds, and 24/7 guarded security.
